Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

This document outlines the data acquisition, retention, and security protocols governing Minecraft Monitor.

1. Data Acquisition

To operate effectively, the Bot indexes the absolute minimum telemetry required for functionality:

  • Guild (Server) IDs: Maintained to cross-reference environmental configurations and monitoring preferences.
  • Channel IDs: Indexed to manage and update designated voice and text endpoints.
  • User IDs: Processed ephemerally to validate administrative privileges and enforce rate-limiting architectures.
  • Node Addresses: IP addresses, domain names, and port structures of monitored Minecraft servers.

Zero-Content Policy: Minecraft Monitor does not index, read, or cache standard user messages or private communications within your Discord server.

Acquired data is utilized exclusively to:
